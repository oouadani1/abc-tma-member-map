Data sources

Organizations: Airtable (ABC TMA base), 2027 Dues and Organizations tables. Last updated –; refreshed automatically every 6 hours.

Routes, stops and stations: MBTA V3 API (GTFS schedule data) for subway, Silver Line, commuter rail, bus and ferry.

Next departures: MBTA V3 API (GTFS-realtime predictions), live.

Bluebikes: Bluebikes GBFS feeds. Station names and docks from station_information; bikes and open docks from station_status, live.

Bike lanes, shared-use paths, walking trails: MAPC AllTrails map service (existing facilities).

Walk and bike networks: OpenStreetMap street data (Geofabrik Massachusetts extract).

Basemap: CARTO Positron and Dark Matter, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Map software: MapLibre GL JS.
