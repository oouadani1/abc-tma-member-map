# ABC TMA Member Map

This map shows where A Better City's TMA member organizations are and how they connect to transit. It answers a simple question: how many members can walk to a train, a bus, or a bikeshare station? The map shows member organizations, MBTA subway, Silver Line, commuter rail, bus, and ferry routes and stops, Bluebikes stations, bike lanes, and trails. Four tiles show the share of members within 50, 250, 500, or 1,000 meters of each kind of stop. Distances are measured along actual streets and paths.

Clicking a member draws its walk or bike network for 5, 10, or 15 minutes. Walking is 80 meters per minute. Biking is 250 meters per minute. The bike network uses any street or path that allows bikes, including roads without bike lanes. A glossary in the map explains the speeds and data sources.

Member data comes from Airtable and updates every 6 hours. Dues are in a password-protected section and are encrypted in the data file. Routes, stops, and departures come from the MBTA. Bluebikes data comes from Bluebikes. Bike lanes and trails come from MAPC. Streets come from OpenStreetMap. The map is one web page built with MapLibre GL JS and hosted on GitHub Pages.
